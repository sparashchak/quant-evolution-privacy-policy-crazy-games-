Privacy Policy

Overview

Quant Evolution is a free arcade game developed by Sviatoslav Parashchak, published on CrazyGames. This policy explains what data is collected, why, and how you can control it.

This version of the game runs in a web browser via CrazyGames and does not require account creation. Gameplay is fully available without providing any personal data.

Data We Collect

This web version of Quant Evolution does not independently collect personal data. The following data may be collected by the CrazyGames platform when you play:

DataPurposeController
Leaderboard scoreGlobal rankingCrazyGames
CrazyGames account info (if logged in)User identity on leaderboardCrazyGames
Ad interaction dataServing video adsCrazyGames / ad partners
Local high scorePersonal best, stored in browserDevice only

Local high score data is stored in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted to our servers.

How Data Is Used

Data collected through the CrazyGames platform is used exclusively to:

  • Display your score on the global leaderboard
  • Serve rewarded and midgame video advertisements
  • Measure gameplay session analytics (session length, retention)

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties beyond what is necessary to operate the services above.

Advertising

Quant Evolution on CrazyGames contains video advertisements (rewarded and midgame) served by the CrazyGames ad platform and its partners. Ads are integrated via the CrazyGames SDK v3.

  • Rewarded ads — optional, watched in exchange for an extra life
  • Midgame ads — shown after a game session ends

Ad data collection and targeting is governed by CrazyGames' Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Quant Evolution is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The game does not require account creation or any personal information to play. If you believe a child's data has been collected without parental consent, please contact us at the email below.

Data Retention

Local high score data stored in your browser can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Leaderboard scores and account data managed by CrazyGames are subject to their own retention policy.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
